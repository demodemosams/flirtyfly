Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about our referral program

When do I receive my referral reward? +
Your ₹50 reward is credited instantly to your FlirtyFly wallet as soon as your referred friend completes their first subscription purchase. There's no waiting period.
Is there a limit on how many friends I can refer? +
Absolutely not! There's no cap on referrals. Refer 1 friend or 1,000 friends — you'll earn ₹50 for every single valid subscription. Some of our top users earn over ₹2,000/month!
How do I withdraw my earnings? +
You can withdraw your referral earnings to any UPI ID or bank account once your balance reaches a minimum of ₹100. Withdrawals are processed within 24–48 business hours.
What counts as a valid referral? +
A referral is valid when: (1) the referred person signs up using your unique code, (2) they are a new user who hasn't registered before, and (3) they purchase any premium subscription plan within 7 days of signing up.
Can I refer myself with a different account? +
No. Self-referrals, multiple accounts, or any form of fraudulent referral activity is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate account suspension and forfeiture of all earnings.
Do referral rewards expire? +
Referral rewards do not expire as long as your account remains active. However, if your account is suspended or deleted, any pending or unclaimed rewards will be forfeited.
