Section 01
Information We Collect

When you use FlirtyFly, we collect certain information to provide and improve our services. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, mobile number, and password when you register
  • Profile Data: Bio, profile photo, and any information you voluntarily add
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, call history, and session duration
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser, and IP address
  • Payment Information: Transaction records (we do not store full card details)
  • Communication Data: Messages or support requests you send us
📌 We only collect information that is necessary to provide you with the best experience on FlirtyFly. We never collect unnecessary personal data.
⚙️
Section 02
How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to operate and improve FlirtyFly. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Create and manage your account securely
  • Connect you with hosts for live video calls
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send you important service notifications and updates
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Improve platform performance and user experience
  • Respond to your support queries and feedback

We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing, sell it to third parties, or use it for purposes unrelated to our services.

🤝
Section 03
Data Sharing & Disclosure

FlirtyFly does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted vendors who help us operate the platform (hosting, payments, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Safety: To protect the rights, safety, or property of FlirtyFly, our users, or the public
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred with prior notice to you
🚫 We will never sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for commercial purposes.
🍪
Section 04
Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on FlirtyFly. These include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core functionality like keeping you logged in
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the platform
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the platform. We do not use cookies for cross-site advertising tracking.

🛡️
Section 05
Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • All data transmitted is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Video calls are encrypted end-to-end using WebRTC
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorised personnel only
⚠️ While we take every precaution, no system is 100% secure. If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, please contact us immediately at security@flirtyfly.in.
⚖️
Section 06
Your Rights

You have full control over your personal data. You can exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information via your Profile settings
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@flirtyfly.in. We will respond within 30 days.

🧒
Section 07
Children's Privacy

FlirtyFly is strictly intended for users aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we discover that a user under 18 has created an account, we will immediately terminate the account and delete all associated data.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used our platform, please contact us immediately at support@flirtyfly.in.

📹
Section 08
Call Recording Policy

FlirtyFly does not record, store, or monitor live video calls between users and hosts. All video calls are peer-to-peer and encrypted via WebRTC.

  • No video or audio recordings are stored on our servers
  • Screen recording or unauthorized capture of calls by users is strictly prohibited
  • Any violation may result in immediate account suspension
  • Call metadata (duration, timestamp, host ID) is stored for billing and safety purposes only
💳
Section 09
Payment Data

All payment processing is handled by trusted third-party payment gateways. FlirtyFly does not store your full card number, CVV, or banking credentials on our servers.

  • Payment transactions are encrypted and processed by PCI-DSS compliant providers
  • We store only the transaction reference ID, amount, and timestamp for billing records
  • All subscription plans are non-refundable as stated in our Terms of Service
🔐 Your financial information is handled exclusively by our payment partners. FlirtyFly never has direct access to your card or bank details.
