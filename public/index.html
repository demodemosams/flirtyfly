❓ FAQ

Questions, Answered

The things people ask us most before they start.

Is FlirtyFly 💋 free to try?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and every new account gets one free minute of live video. The free minute is valid one time only. After that you'll need a plan to keep calling.

Do I need to install an app?

No. FlirtyFly 💋 works in your browser on phone, tablet and laptop. You'll just be asked to allow your camera and microphone when a call starts.

How do I start a call?

Sign up, open the Discover page, tap a host who is online and press "Start video call". The host gets your call straight away.

Which plans are available?

We offer weekly and monthly plans, including unlimited-call options. The current plans and prices are listed under Pricing.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are non-refundable, so please use your free minute to try the experience first. The full details are in our Terms of Service.

How can I become a host?

Fill in the short host application on this page and our team will contact you on your mobile. Already a host? Log in here.

I need help with my account. Who do I contact?

Email flirtyfly.support@gmail.com or use the contact form below. Our support team responds within 24 hours.
